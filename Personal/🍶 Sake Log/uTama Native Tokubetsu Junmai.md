Notes: "Loved this — on my 3rd bottle. Orchid Sake Brewery is Singapore's first and only sake brewery (est. 2024, Jurong Food Hub). Head brewer Yumika Yamamoto trained for ~8 years in Japan before returning to brew here. uTama is a double name: Sang Nila Utama (legendary founder of Singapura) + 'tama' (魂, Japanese for 'spirit/soul'). What makes it special: blended with Black Sarawak glutinous rice (pulut hitam) from Borneo, giving it a naturally stunning berry-red/rose hue — no added colouring. Brewed intentionally creamier and more acidic than Japanese junmai to pair with bolder SEA flavours like curry. Nose: rose, white flowers, grape. Palate: melon, pulut hitam sweetness, berry, grape, light roasty finish. Gentle clean acidity. Regular SGD $95, typically ~$76 on sale / first-order discount. Sister variant uTama Tropic (Junmai Daiginjo) won Silver at Tokyo Sake Challenge 2025."
