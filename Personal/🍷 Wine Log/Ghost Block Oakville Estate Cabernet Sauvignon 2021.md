Plush and smooth with refined tannins — classic Oakville. Drink now through 2038. Scores: WS 94pts, CellarTracker 92.6, Vivino 4.3/5. US retail ~USD $82-87 — SGD $110 is genuinely competitive given import duties and 9% GST. Listed at Wine Connection as 'Oakville Cabernet Sauvignon Napa Valley.'"
