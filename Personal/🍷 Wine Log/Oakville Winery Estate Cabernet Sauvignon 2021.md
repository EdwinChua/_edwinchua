Notes: "Absolutely loved this. 'Oakville Winery' is the mid-tier estate label from Ghost Block Estate Wines (Pelissa/Hoxsey family) — a five-generation Napa farming dynasty since 1903. Vineyard is directly behind the winery, across the road from Opus One. 650 acres all certified organic. ~95% Cab Sav, 3% Malbec, 2% Petit Verdot. 24 months in French oak (~50% new). Nose: blackberry, cassis, plum, dusty earth, cedar, rose petal. Palate: boysenberry, black cherry, cocoa, toffee, vanilla cream, tobacco. Satiny ultra-refined tannins, vibrant acidity, long elegant finish. WE 94pts: 'merits comparison with far more expensive Oakville neighbors.' Drink now through 2029+. US retail ~USD $45-57; SGD $110 reflects normal Singapore import duties and 9% GST. The premium Ghost Block label (same estate, higher tier) fetches USD $85+."
