**🥗 Anti-inflammatory foods (omega-3s + polyphenols)**

- Walnuts: ~28 g/day (¼ cup)
- Fatty fish: salmon, sardines, mackerel
- Green tea: 3–4 cups/day
- Leafy greens and berries

**🥦 Insulin sensitivity foods**

- Broccoli sprouts: ~½ cup daily, raw (small daily dose > weekly loading)
- 85%+ dark chocolate
- Fermented foods: kimchi, sauerkraut

**🫒 Fat-burning signal foods**

- Extra virgin olive oil: ~1.5 tbsp (25 mL) daily — swap in for other oils

**🏃 Exercise**

- 2–3 sessions/week, ~45 min each
- Moderate-to-vigorous intensity (HIIT or vigorous aerobic > resistance training for visceral fat specifically)
- Talk test: can speak in sentences but can't sing = moderate
- Ramp up gradually; consistency > occasional intensity

**🧠 Mindset**

- Don't crash-diet — your body fights back (slower metabolism, more hunger)
- Aim for a small calorie deficit, not starvation
- Ignore the scale — visceral fat can drop dramatically while weight barely moves
- Exercise dose-response has no ceiling; more = more fat lost

**⏱️ Realistic timeline**

- Meaningful visceral fat reduction in 8–12 weeks if consistent
